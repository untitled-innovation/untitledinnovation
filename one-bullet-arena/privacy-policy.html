QUICK SUMMARY: PRIVACY AT A GLANCE
  • ✔ No Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or sell any personal data, usage data, or telemetry.
  • ✔ Offline & Local Multiplayer: The game is built to work offline and let you play with friends locally.
  • ✔ Custom Images Stay On Your Phone: Any custom image or avatar upload happens strictly inside your phone. It is shared only with friends on your local Wi-Fi during gameplay and is never transmitted to any external or cloud server.
  • ✔ No Accounts or Tracking: No registration, no ads, and no third-party trackers.

01 — INTRODUCTION

Untitled Innovations ("we," "us," or "our") is the developer of the mobile game One Bullet Arena (the "Game"). We believe user privacy is a fundamental right.

One Bullet Arena is built from the ground up on an offline-first, zero-knowledge, zero-data-collection architecture. This Privacy Policy details our practices and confirms that your personal information and gameplay stay entirely on your device and within your local sessions.

02 — ZERO DATA COLLECTION

We do NOT collect, harvest, aggregate, store, transmit, or share any personal information from users of One Bullet Arena. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not require user accounts, passwords, or cloud sign-in.
  • We do not collect device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, IDFA, or MAC address).
  • We do not track geolocation or GPS coordinates.
  • We do not collect gameplay statistics, telemetry, analytics, or behavioral profiles.
  • We do not maintain any centralized user databases or analytics servers.

03 — OFFLINE & LOCAL WI-FI MULTIPLAYER

One Bullet Arena is designed to function completely offline without requiring any internet connection or cloud service:

  • Offline Play: You can play the game anywhere without cellular data or internet connectivity.
  • Playing with Friends over Local Wi-Fi: Multiplayer matches with friends take place over your local Wi-Fi network (or portable Wi-Fi hotspot). Game traffic, lobby discovery, and match updates are exchanged directly between connected devices over your local area network (LAN / peer-to-peer).
  • No Cloud Routing: No multiplayer traffic, game actions, or session details are routed through, stored by, or intercepted by our servers or any third-party relay.

04 — CUSTOM IMAGE UPLOADS & ON-DEVICE HANDLING

One Bullet Arena allows players to personalize their gameplay experience with custom images or avatars:

  • Stays on Your Phone: When you select or upload a custom image/avatar within the Game, the image processing and storage happen exclusively inside your phone's local storage.
  • Local Wi-Fi Transmission Only: When playing in a local multiplayer match with friends, your selected avatar/image is transmitted directly to your friends' devices over the local Wi-Fi network solely for the duration of the in-game display.
  • No External / Cloud Upload: Your images are never uploaded to, hosted on, or processed by any external cloud server. Untitled Innovations has no access to your gallery, camera roll, or uploaded images.

05 — DEVICE PERMISSIONS

To enable core game features, One Bullet Arena may request the following device permissions:

  • Photos / Storage Permission: Requested solely to enable you to select a custom avatar or image from your local device. Images selected remain on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi Permission: Requested solely to discover and connect with nearby devices hosted by friends on the same local Wi-Fi network for multiplayer games.

These permissions are utilized solely on-device for the exact features you activate, and are never used to harvest data.

06 — THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & ANALYTICS

One Bullet Arena does NOT integrate:

  • Any third-party advertising SDKs or ad networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads)
  • Any analytics or tracking SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer)
  • Any third-party social media tracking plugins

The app does not communicate with external tracking partners.

07 — CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Because One Bullet Arena collects no personal data whatsoever from any user, it does not collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under applicable age thresholds in your jurisdiction, such as COPPA in the United States or GDPR in the European Union).

The Game is completely safe for players of all ages.

08 — DATA RETENTION & REMOVAL

Because we do not store any of your data on servers, there is no remote data to retain:

  • All local preferences, settings, and custom images reside strictly in your device's sandboxed storage.
  • You can permanently remove all local game data at any time by clearing the application cache/data in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

09 — CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically if new features or technical updates are introduced. Any updates will be published at https://untitledinnovations.com/one-bullet-arena/privacy-policy.html with an updated "Last updated" date.
