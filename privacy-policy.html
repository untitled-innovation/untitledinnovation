01 — INTRODUCTION

Untitled Innovations ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Zero Layer VPN mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Zero Layer VPN is designed with a strict no-logs policy — we do not monitor, record, or store your VPN browsing activity.

02 — INFORMATION WE COLLECT

a) Account Data (optional)

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, we receive your display name, email address and profile photo URL through Firebase Authentication. We store the following in a user record in Google Cloud Firestore: a unique user ID, your name, your email address and the date your account was created.

This information is used to authenticate you, to display your profile in the App, and to link your Pro subscription and Premium server access to your account.

b) Subscription & Purchase Data

If you buy or restore a Zero Layer Pro subscription, purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage your entitlement. RevenueCat receives your account user ID, purchase and subscription details (product, price, purchase and renewal dates, entitlement status) and an identifier for your device or app install. We never receive or store your payment card details.

c) Premium Server Access Data

If you use Premium Secured (WireGuard) servers, a WireGuard key slot is assigned to your user ID and stored in Firestore, along with the time it was claimed. This is used only to give your device its own tunnel identity and to release it when you rotate your key or delete your account.

d) IP Address & Approximate Location

To show your current public IP address and country, the App sends a request to a third-party IP lookup service (GeoJS). That service necessarily sees the IP address the request comes from (your real IP when disconnected, or the VPN server's IP when connected). We do not store this result on any server.

e) Speed Test

When you run the speed test, the App downloads and uploads test data to and from Cloudflare speed-test endpoints, which can see your IP address as part of that request. No test content is stored by us.

f) Data Stored on Your Device

Your preferences, selected server, split-tunneling app selection, cached server lists, a lifetime data-usage counter and your last-known Pro status are stored locally on your device. The data-usage counter is a readout only and is never sent to us.

g) VPN Connection Data

We do NOT collect, log, or store:

  • Your browsing history or traffic data
  • DNS queries
  • The websites or services you access through the VPN
  • Connection timestamps or session duration
  • Bandwidth usage per session

Please note that the operators of the VPN servers you connect to (see Third-Party Services) may have their own logging practices, which we do not control.

h) Device Information

We do not run analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect device model or operating system telemetry for our own purposes. The third-party services listed below may receive limited technical data as described in their own policies.

03 — HOW WE USE INFORMATION

The information described above is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating your identity via Google Sign-In
  • Displaying your profile within the App
  • Validating purchases and managing your Pro entitlement
  • Assigning your device a Premium WireGuard key slot
  • Showing your IP address, country and speed-test results

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

04 — THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The following third parties process data on our behalf or as part of the App's features. Each is governed by its own privacy policy.

a) Google Sign-In & Firebase (Authentication and Firestore)

Used for sign-in and to store the account and key-slot records described above. policies.google.com/privacy  |  firebase.google.com/support/privacy

b) RevenueCat

Used to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your user ID, purchase and subscription data, and device/app identifiers as described in section 02(b). revenuecat.com/privacy

c) Google Play Billing

Processes payments for Pro subscriptions. Google Payments privacy notice

d) GeoJS

Provides the IP address and country lookup. geojs.io

e) Cloudflare

Hosts the speed-test endpoints. cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

f) VPN Gate & VPNBook

The App can connect to volunteer-run servers from the VPN Gate project (vpngate.net) and to servers provided by VPNBook. These are operated independently and we do not control them. Please refer to the VPN Gate policy and VPNBook's own terms for their data practices. Traffic you send through these servers is visible to their operators.

05 — DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

Preferences and settings are stored locally on your device. Account and key-slot records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, and subscription records are held by RevenueCat. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

We implement industry-standard security practices, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

06 — ANALYTICS & ADVERTISING

Zero Layer VPN does NOT use analytics SDKs, crash-reporting tools, or advertising networks, and we do not track your behavior within or outside the App. The only third-party SDKs that receive user data are those listed in section 04, and only for the purposes stated there.

07 — CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

08 — DATA RETENTION & DELETION

  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data
  • Account records in Firestore are kept until you request deletion
  • Subscription records held by RevenueCat are retained as needed to provide your entitlement and meet legal and accounting obligations
  • You can sign out at any time, and request complete account deletion via our Account Deletion page, which also releases your Premium key slot

09 — YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you (account name, email and user ID)
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for Google Sign-In at any time
  • Use the App without signing in (with limited features)

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

10 — CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
