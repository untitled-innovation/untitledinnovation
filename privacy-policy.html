01 — INTRODUCTION

Untitled Innovations ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Zero Layer VPN mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Zero Layer VPN is designed with a strict no-logs policy — we do not monitor, record, or store your VPN browsing activity.

02 — INFORMATION WE COLLECT

a) Google Sign-In Data

When you sign in using Google, we receive the following from your Google account:

  • Display name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL

This information is used solely for authentication and managing future subscription services. It is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external server owned by us.

b) VPN Connection Data

We do NOT collect, log, or store:

  • Your browsing history or traffic data
  • DNS queries
  • IP addresses used during VPN sessions
  • Connection timestamps or session duration
  • Bandwidth usage per session

c) Device Information

We do not collect device identifiers, hardware model information, operating system versions, or any telemetry data.

03 — HOW WE USE INFORMATION

The limited information we access is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating your identity via Google Sign-In
  • Displaying your profile within the App
  • Managing future subscription entitlements

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties.

04 — THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

a) Google Sign-In

Our App uses Google Sign-In for authentication. When you sign in, Google's own privacy policy governs the data they collect. You can review it at: policies.google.com/privacy

b) VPN Gate

The App utilizes volunteer-run VPN servers provided by the VPN Gate project (vpngate.net). Each relay server is operated independently by volunteers. We do not control these servers. Please refer to the VPN Gate policy for details on their data practices.

05 — DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

All user preferences and authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using encrypted shared preferences. We do not operate backend servers that store user data.

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect data stored on your device, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

06 — ANALYTICS & ADVERTISING

Zero Layer VPN does NOT use any analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or advertising networks. We do not track your behavior within or outside the App.

07 — CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

08 — DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data
  • You can sign out of Google at any time to revoke authentication
  • You can request complete account deletion via our Account Deletion page

09 — YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal information we hold (limited to Google profile data stored locally)
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for Google Sign-In at any time
  • Use the App without signing in (with limited features)

10 — CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
