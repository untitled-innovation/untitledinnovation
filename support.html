01 — COMMON ISSUES & TROUBLESHOOTING

Cannot connect to a VPN server?

Zero Layer VPN relies on volunteer-run servers. If a connection fails or is slow:

  • The specific server might be overloaded or offline.
  • Try connecting to a different country or server.
  • Use the "Auto Connect" feature to find the best available server.

App crashes on launch?

  • Ensure you are running the latest version from the Play Store.
  • Clear the app's cache in your Android settings.
