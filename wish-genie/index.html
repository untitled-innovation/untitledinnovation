FAQ

Good questions.

What is WISH Genie?

A wishlist app that works across stores. You save products you want, and Genie tracks prices and finds offers for them.

Which stores does it work with?

Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio and more. Paste a link or share a product from the store’s app.

How do price alerts work?

Turn on “Alert me on price drops” for a wish and set a target price. Genie notifies you when the price drops.

Does it cost anything?

No. WISH Genie is free to use.

How does WISH Genie make money?

When you tap through to a store and buy, some of our links are affiliate links through networks such as Cuelinks. The store may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes the price you pay, and we show offers based on what is useful to you.
